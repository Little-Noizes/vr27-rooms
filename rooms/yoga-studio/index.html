Controls and notes

Drag to look around. W and S (or the up and down arrows) walk, A and D step sideways, Q and E (or left and right arrows) turn. Click an IR point in the room or on the plan to sit there. Space plays or stops.

Treatment steps: none (masonry, timber floor, corrugated metal soffit); acoustic curtains on both long walls; plus Autex QuietSpace 100 mm direct fixed to the soffit; plus QuietSpace 25 mm wall panels on the rear walls. Switching changes the IRs and the model together.

IRs simulated in Treble (hybrid, wave solver to 710 Hz), 2nd order ambisonic, played here at 1st order. One level reference across all scenarios and sources. Speakers modelled as Genelec 8030A directivity (stand-in for the KRK Classic 5), teacher as a speech source.

Each speaker has its own IR set, so L only, R only and both are true one- or two-speaker renderings (no level compensation: one speaker is about 3 dB quieter, as in the room). L_spk.wav feeds the left speaker and R_spk.wav the right. (Current files are identical, so the music is effectively mono.) Sliders at their minimum switch that sound off completely. Position switches to the nearest IR point (the grid is not rectangular, so no 4-point blend).
