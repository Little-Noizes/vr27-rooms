Controls and notes

Drag to look around. W and S (or the up and down arrows) walk, A and D step sideways, Q and E (or left and right arrows) turn. Click a grid point in the room or on the plan to stand there. Space plays or stops.

Left or Right plays the mono mix from that monitor only. Stereo sends the left channel to the left monitor and the right channel to the right. Mono both plays the mono mix from both at −3 dB each.

The scan is shown at 1.17 × its built size, the scale adopted on 31 Oct 2026. The grid sits 0.5 m further forward than the fit placed it, so the rear row is about 0.5 m off the rear wall. The music is MP3 for this page; the IRs are the converted AmbiX set, 16-bit.
