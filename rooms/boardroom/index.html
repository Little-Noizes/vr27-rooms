Controls and notes

Drag to look around. W and S (or the up and down arrows) walk, A and D step sideways, Q and E (or left and right arrows) turn. Click a grid point in the room or on the plan to stand there. Space plays or stops.

Switching scene changes the IRs, the lighting and the curtain together. The curtain runs along the window wall and the stage-end wall, so it also cuts the daylight.

Both IR sets are simulated (2019) and share one level reference, so switching changes only the room. The grid sits centred in the room; the source lands on the presenter's position at the stage end. Eye and ear height 1.2 m (seated).

Model rebuilt from the 2019 Unity FBX files, lighting baked in Blender Cycles for curtain off and on. Plants and the presenter figure are left out.
