Controls and notes

Drag to look around. W and S (or the up and down arrows) walk, A and D step sideways, Q and E (or left and right arrows) turn. Click an IR point in the room or on the plan to stand there. Space plays or stops.

Ceiling: walls plasterboard, floor linoleum. Plasterboard ceiling throughout, or the perimeter ceiling zone (32.3 m²) in Bioguard Acoustic 19 mm with the laminar flow field unchanged. The scan does not change with the ceiling option.

Equipment (ventilator, laminar flow HVAC, suction) and staff voices are pre-convolved per IR point (baked loops), so they switch with no reverb build-up. Monitors and the ceiling PA are convolved live. Position switches to the nearest IR point on the 1 m grid.

IRs simulated in Treble (hybrid, wave solver to 710 Hz), 2nd order ambisonic, played here at 1st order. Treble normalises each source's IRs, so the balance between sources is set by the sliders, not calibrated. Each source is level-matched between the two ceilings. Staff voices are placeholders.
